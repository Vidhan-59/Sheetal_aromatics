## 2. Who we are

Sheetal Aromatics was founded in **2005 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat** as a partnership firm trading in aromatic chemicals and related products. The firm is run directly by its two partners, **Shailesh Shah** and **Vidhan Shah** — enquiries are answered by the people who source the material, not routed through a sales queue.

**How the business grew (as told on the About page):**

| Phase | What happened |
|---|---|
| Established (2005) | Founded in Ahmedabad as a partnership firm trading in aromatic chemicals |
| Industry experience | Steady work with fragrance compounders, flavour houses and formulators built product knowledge and supplier relationships |
| Product expansion | Range widened into essential oils, Ayurvedic herbs and powders, high-purity metals and pharma intermediates |
| Export focus | Importer Exporter Code (IEC 0805014608) supports buyers outside India alongside domestic supply |
| Today | 88 catalogued products across 7 categories, plus phase transfer catalysts and food & pharma ingredients |

**Our operating principles** (published on the site):

1. **Say what we can actually supply** — if a grade or quantity can't be delivered reliably, we say so at enquiry stage.
2. **Work from the buyer's specification** — we quote against the buyer's grade, assay and limits, not "whatever is closest".
3. **Keep the same people on the account** — buyers deal with the partners throughout.
4. **Treat documentation as part of the product** — COA, SDS and export paperwork are confirmed before an order.

**Business model:** **trading + custom manufacturing.** Most of the catalogue is sourced and supplied (trading); when a buyer needs a product made to their own specification, the firm takes it up as custom manufacturing. Website copy says exactly this; product structured data does not claim manufacture (a per-product `madeInHouse` flag exists for any product the firm makes itself).

**Positioning:** a specification-led, export-capable B2B trader and custom manufacturer from Gujarat with direct partner contact, strong technical product information online, and a broad range spanning specialty chemicals and natural products.

## 9. How we sell: enquiry to delivery

### The five-step process (as published)
1. **Enquiry** — buyer sends product, quantity, specification and destination.
2. **Confirmation** — we confirm what can be supplied and against which specification.
3. **Quotation** — pricing, packing and terms quoted for the requirement.
4. **Documentation** — paperwork prepared for the agreed terms and destination.
5. **Despatch** — shipment coordinated and tracked to delivery.

### What a buyer should send
| Detail | Why |
|---|---|
| Product name (+ synonym or CAS number) | Removes ambiguity between trade names |
| Quantity — trial and regular | Trial and contract volumes are quoted differently |
| Specification — grade, assay, marker, impurity limits | We quote against the buyer's spec |
| Packing — pack size, material, labelling | Changes price and handling |
| Destination — port/city and Incoterm | Drives freight, documents and mode (air/sea) |
| End use | Confirms suitability and any handling/regulatory constraints |

### Documents, packing, shipping
- **Documents on request per enquiry:** certificate of analysis (COA), safety data sheet (SDS/MSDS, GHS 16-section), method of analysis, certificate of origin; for exports also commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading / air waybill; DG declaration for hazardous goods.
- **Packing:** confirmed per order (typical export packs: HDPE/MS drums or IBCs for liquids, lined bags or fibre drums for powders, palletised and stretch-wrapped; UN-certified packaging for dangerous goods).
- **Ports:** Mundra, Kandla (Deendayal), Hazira, or Nhava Sheva (JNPT). **Incoterms:** EXW Ahmedabad, FOB Indian port, CFR/CIF buyer's port.
- **Samples:** sample → trial lot → regular orders is the usual sequence for new buyers.
- **Restricted / hazardous items** (e.g. sodium metal): handling, packing and transport agreed per order before despatch.

### Enquiry handling system (website)
- Every web enquiry gets a **reference number** (format `SA-YYMMDD-XXXX`, e.g. `SA-261002-6JUL`).
- **Team email** to sheetalaromatics@gmail.com with all fields, the page it came from, and *Reply to buyer* / *WhatsApp buyer* buttons.
- **Buyer confirmation email** (branded, quotes the reference) — currently paused until the domain is verified in Resend (see §11).
- Spam protection: honeypot field, minimum fill time, rate limit (5 per IP per 10 minutes).

## 10. Content & accuracy rules

These rules apply to the website, ads, emails, listings and anything written about the company:

- **Never invent** prices, MOQ, stock, lead times, certifications, capacities, staff numbers, customer names, testimonials, ratings, reviews or countries served.
- **CAS numbers, molecular formulas and weights** are public identifiers verified against PubChem — not claims about our material.
- **Typical grade** is indicative; final specification is confirmed per order. Technical notes on product pages are labelled "general information".
- **Images** are either PubChem-based structures or credited, licensed photos; representative photos are labelled as such — never presented as our own stock.
- **No health claims** for Ayurvedic products (never "treats", "cures", "boosts").
- **Dangerous goods** (sodium metal, etc.) are never promoted in paid social ads.
- **Business model wording:** "trading and custom manufacturing". Never call the firm a manufacturer of the whole range, and never claim a specific product is manufactured in-house unless the owner confirms it (`madeInHouse`).
- **Export reach:** "5+ countries" — name the countries only once the owner lists them.
- **No fake schema** (no invented ratings or prices to win rich results), no doorway "supplier in {city}" pages, no machine-translated pages.

## 17. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CAS number** | Unique Chemical Abstracts Service identifier for a substance (e.g. 100-51-6 = benzyl alcohol) |
| **COA** | Certificate of Analysis — lab results for a specific batch |
| **SDS / MSDS** | Safety Data Sheet — 16-section GHS hazard and handling document |
| **PTC** | Phase transfer catalyst — salt (quaternary ammonium/phosphonium) that carries reactants between water and organic phases |
| **TBAB / TEBAC / TBAHS / BTPPC** | Tetrabutylammonium bromide / benzyltriethylammonium chloride / tetrabutylammonium hydrogen sulphate / benzyltriphenylphosphonium chloride |
| **Wittig reagent** | Phosphonium salt used to make carbon–carbon double bonds (olefination) |
| **IP / BP / USP / EP** | Indian / British / US / European Pharmacopoeia grades |
| **FCC / E-number** | Food Chemicals Codex grade / EU food-additive code (E211 = sodium benzoate) |
| **GST / IEC** | Indian Goods & Services Tax registration / Importer Exporter Code |
| **RCMC** | Registration-cum-Membership Certificate from an export promotion council |
| **CHEMEXCIL / SHEFEXIL / AYUSHEXCIL** | Export councils for chemicals / shellac & forest products / AYUSH products |
| **HS code** | Harmonised System customs classification code |
| **Incoterms** | Delivery terms — EXW (ex works), FOB (free on board), CFR/CIF (cost & freight / + insurance) |
| **DG / UN number / IMDG** | Dangerous goods / UN hazard identifier / sea dangerous-goods code |
| **REACH / TSCA** | EU and US chemical regulations |
| **FSSAI** | Food Safety and Standards Authority of India |
| **NAP** | Name, Address, Phone — must match everywhere for local SEO |
| **GBP / GSC / GA4** | Google Business Profile / Google Search Console / Google Analytics 4 |
