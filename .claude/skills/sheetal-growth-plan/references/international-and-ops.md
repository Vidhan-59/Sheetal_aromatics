## Focus beats coverage

Selling 88 catalogued products (500+ available) into every market at once spreads a two-partner firm too thin. Today's reach is 5+ countries. Prove the hero lines in a few markets, then expand.

| Line | Beachhead markets | Why | Must-have before selling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Phase transfer catalysts & phosphonium salts | India (Gujarat/Maharashtra/Telangana pharma & agro clusters) · Southeast Asia · Middle East | Technical, repeat consumption; Gujarat is a recognised PTC hub; open search results for phosphonium salts | Spec sheet per product, typical COA, GHS SDS, moisture-proof packing |
| 2 · Aroma chemicals (Nerolin, benzyl esters, methyl salicylate) | UAE (re-export hub to Africa & Central Asia) · East & West Africa soap/detergent makers · Indonesia/Malaysia | Soap/detergent demand; price-competitive vs Europe; short transit from Mundra | GC-based COA, odour-match samples, Halal certificate for Muslim-majority markets |
| 3 · Ayurvedic botanicals (ashwagandha root, shilajit, moringa) | USA (largest, testing-heavy) · GCC · Southeast Asia — **not EU supplement channels for now** | Origin advantage China can't copy | NABL-lab heavy-metal, microbial & pesticide reports per lot; root-only ashwagandha; no health claims |
| Supporting · food preservatives | Kenya, Tanzania, Nigeria, Gulf distributors | Container volume filler alongside aroma chemicals | FSSAI licence, food-grade COA (FCC / E-number specs) |
| Supporting · pharma intermediates | Indian API makers first, then regulated markets via CDMOs | High value, low online competition | Impurity profile, method of analysis, change-control commitment |

## Market evidence (public, partly secondary)
- Essential oils (HS 3301): India exported ~US$909M in 2023; top buyers USA 28%, China 10%, Malaysia 8%, then France, Germany, UAE, Mexico, Indonesia, Netherlands, UK. ~US$929M reported for FY2024-25.
- Chemicals: CHEMEXCIL reported organic + inorganic exports ~US$29.3B in FY2024; Indian organic chemical exports to the USA ~US$2.56B in 2024 (secondary).
- AYUSH & herbal: ~US$689M in FY2024-25 (+~6%).
- Pull country × HS-code detail for your own products before choosing markets.

## Regulatory watch-list (Oct 2026)
| Topic | Status | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Ashwagandha in EU supplements | Denmark banned (2023); Netherlands draft ban in consultation until 7 Dec 2026; no EU-wide ban; UK FSA review pending | Don't build EU supplement sales on ashwagandha; prioritise US & GCC |
| US FDA on Ayurvedic products | Heavy-metal warnings; import alert allows detention without examination | Every US botanical shipment needs credible heavy-metal data |
| Shilajit | EU: reportedly novel food outside supplements (check EU Novel Food Catalogue); US: heavy metals, California Prop 65 | Sell only with test reports, to buyers who handle their market's compliance |
| EU REACH | Importer registers at ≥ 1 t/yr, or non-EU supplier appoints an Only Representative; SDS at any volume | Ask EU buyers if they hold registration; offer GHS SDS |
| UK REACH | Transitional deadlines extended to 2029–2031 (in force 6 Aug 2026) | UK currently easier than EU for new chemical suppliers |
| Meta ad policy | No hazardous goods; supplement ads restricted | Chemicals & metals via LinkedIn, email, fairs |
| India DPDP Rules | Notified Nov 2025; most obligations from ~May 2027 | Prepare consent and data handling |

## Export-readiness kit (build once, use in every channel)
**Documents:** spec sheet + typical COA per hero product · GHS 16-section SDS per product · 2-page company profile PDF · price sheet (FOB Mundra/Nhava Sheva; CIF for 5 target ports) · sample policy (size, who pays courier) · standard terms (e.g. advance + balance against BL copy, or LC at sight).

**Credentials (only real ones):** RCMC — CHEMEXCIL (chemicals, essential oils), SHEFEXIL or AYUSHEXCIL (herbs) · ISO 9001 · FSSAI licence if trading food ingredients · Halal / Kosher for food & fragrance lines · D-U-N-S number · ECGC cover for credit sales.

## Government support
- **Market Access Support (MAS)** under the Export Promotion Mission (replaced MAI from 2026): trade-fair participation and buyer-seller meets via export councils.
- **TRACE:** reimbursement of testing & certification costs for MSMEs (reported up to 95% for micro & small firms — confirm with DGFT) — useful for heavy-metal tests, ISO, Halal.
- **RoDTEP:** extended to 31 Dec 2026 — claim on eligible export shipments.
- **ECGC:** export credit insurance, enabling credit terms without bad-debt risk.

## Sales operations (what makes marketing pay off)
- **One CRM** (Zoho CRM or HubSpot free): New → Qualified → Spec/Sample → Quoted → Trial order → Repeat. Website enquiries already carry a reference number (`SA-YYMMDD-XXXX`) — use it as the CRM key.
- **Response time:** reply within 4 working hours; quotes in 24–48 h. Speed beats price on first contact with importers.
- **Quote template:** product, CAS, grade, assay, packing, quantity, Incoterm + port, price, validity, payment terms, lead time, documents included.
- **Follow-up** every quote at day 3 and day 10.
- **Weekly 30-minute partner review:** leads by source, quotes out, samples out, orders, lost reasons.
- **One hire when volume justifies it:** export-sales/marketing executive owning LinkedIn, email sequences, listings and the CRM.

## How to adjust
- **Monthly:** shift effort from the channel with the highest cost per qualified lead to the lowest; rewrite the worst email sequence; retitle the 3 pages with the most impressions and lowest CTR (GSC: impressions > 50, CTR < 1%; note the date, compare after 28 days).
- **Quarterly:** keep beachhead markets that produced samples; replace markets with zero positive replies after 300+ contacts.
- **Never** judge SEO on one month; judge outbound only after 200+ contacts per segment.
