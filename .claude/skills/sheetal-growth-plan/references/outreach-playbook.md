## Email marketing

### Two streams — never mix them (mixing burns sender reputation)
| Stream | Audience | Sent from | Tool | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Permission list (newsletter) | Customers, past enquirers, people who ticked the consent box | Main domain (sales@sheetalaromatics.com) | Brevo, Zoho Campaigns or Mailchimp | Monthly |
| 2 · Cold outreach | Researched importers & manufacturers | Separate look-alike domains (check e.g. sheetal-aromatics.com, sheetalaromatics.in) redirecting to the main site | Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist; one-to-one style, plain text | Daily |

### Setup (weeks 1–4)
1. Main-domain mailbox + MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC (seo-playbook §Domain email).
2. Buy 2 outreach domains; redirect each to sheetalaromatics.com; SPF + DKIM + DMARC on each.
3. 2 mailboxes per outreach domain under a real sender's name (e.g. Vidhan Shah, Export Desk).
4. Warm up 3–4 weeks starting at 5–10 emails/day.
5. Steady state: 25–30 per mailbox per day → ~100/day across 4 mailboxes (≈50 new + 50 follow-ups).
6. Verify every address (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce or similar); bounce rate < 2%.

Inbox-provider rules: SPF + DKIM, DMARC, one-click unsubscribe on marketing mail, spam complaints < 0.3% (Google recommends < 0.1%).

### Prospect sources
| Source | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shipment data (Volza, Eximpedia, Seair, Panjiva) | Companies already importing your HS codes from India | Highest quality; paid. Start with one country × one HS code |
| Trade-fair exhibitor lists (CPHI, in-cosmetics, Vitafoods, Chemspec) | Pharma, cosmetics, nutraceutical buyers | Public; find the right person on LinkedIn |
| LinkedIn / Sales Navigator, Apollo | Names & titles (procurement, R&D) | Pairs with the LinkedIn routine |
| Kompass, Europages, national chemical associations | Distributors | Good for preservatives and aroma chemicals |

### Monthly newsletter — "Sheetal Aromatics Supply Note"
Five short items: (1) what's in stock / newly available, (2) one new guide, (3) one logistics update (freight, port or regulation), (4) one product spotlight with CAS and uses, (5) reply-to-quote CTA. From sales@, with one-click unsubscribe.

### Country rules for cold B2B email (general guidance, not legal advice)
| Market | Rule of thumb | Action |
|---|---|---|
| USA | CAN-SPAM opt-out model: accurate headers, physical address, unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days | OK |
| UK | PECR: corporate addresses (Ltd/LLP) don't need consent; sole traders and some partnerships do | Corporate only |
| EU (most) | GDPR legitimate interest can cover relevant B2B messages; targeted, documented, easy opt-out | Careful |
| Germany | UWG §7 requires prior consent even B2B | **Don't** cold email — LinkedIn & fairs |
| Canada | CASL consent-based, strict | Avoid |
| India | DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025, most obligations from ~May 2027 | Use data responsibly; honour opt-outs |
| UAE | PDPL in force; executive regulations reportedly pending | Careful |

### Email KPIs
Per sequence and segment: bounce (< 2%), spam complaints (< 0.1%), reply rate, positive-reply rate, samples sent, quotes sent, orders. Judge on positive replies per 100 contacts after **≥ 200 contacts**; below that it's noise.

## LinkedIn — founder-led routine (70 requests/week)

Buyers respond to people more than company pages: outreach from the partners' personal profiles; the company page is the credibility anchor.

**Limits:** no published fixed cap; ~100/week is the commonly observed ceiling, so 70/week (14/day Mon–Fri) is safe. Personalised notes ≤ ~200 characters; free accounts get few personalised notes per month — consider Premium/Sales Navigator once proven. **No automation tools** (prohibited; risks restriction). Withdraw invitations pending > 3 weeks.

### Profile setup (week 1)
- Headline: "Partner, Sheetal Aromatics · Phase transfer catalysts, aroma chemicals & pharma intermediates · Exporting from Ahmedabad since 2005"
- About: three short paragraphs — who you supply, what (with CAS examples), how to request a quote (link /request-a-quote).
- Featured: PTC guide, importing-from-India guide, RFQ page.
- Company page: logo, banner with real product/packing photos, the 7 product groups, website link; both partners listed as employees. Add the URL to Organization `sameAs`.

### Weekly split (70)
| Segment | Titles | Industries | Requests |
|---|---|---|---|
| PTC & intermediates | Purchase/Procurement/Sourcing Manager, R&D Chemist, Process Chemist, Plant Head | Pharma, chemical manufacturing, agrochemicals | 25 |
| Aroma chemicals | Perfumer, Fragrance Evaluator, Purchase Manager, Formulation Head | Personal care, F&F, soap & detergent | 20 |
| Botanicals | Sourcing Manager, Product Development, Founder | Nutraceuticals, supplements, Ayurvedic | 15 |
| Distributors / importers | Owner, MD, Import Manager | Chemicals wholesale, food-ingredient distribution | 10 |

Geography rotation every 4 weeks: India + UAE → Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand → Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt → USA (botanicals, aroma) → repeat what worked.

### Connection notes (< 200 characters)
- **PTC / pharma:** "Hi {Name}, I'm with Sheetal Aromatics, Ahmedabad. We supply phase transfer catalysts like TBAB and TEBAC. Your work at {Company} caught my eye; glad to connect."
- **Fragrance:** "Hi {Name}, I supply aroma chemicals (Nerolin, benzyl esters, methyl salicylate) from Gujarat to fragrance and soap makers. Would be glad to connect."
- **Botanicals:** "Hi {Name}, I work with Ayurvedic botanicals from India (ashwagandha root, shilajit, moringa) for supplement makers. Happy to connect and share sourcing notes."
- **Distributor:** "Hi {Name}, we're an Ahmedabad chemical supplier (since 2005) working with importers and distributors. Noticed {Company}'s range; glad to connect."

### After they accept
| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | "Thanks for connecting, {Name}. If it's ever useful, I'm the person to ask about {segment} materials from India." (no pitch) |
| Day 4–6 | Share one relevant guide link with a one-line reason it fits their work |
| Day 12–15 | "Are you sourcing any of {3 products} from India today? Happy to quote against your spec or send a sample." |
| Then | Stop; stay visible through posts and comments |

### Content rhythm (partner: 3 posts/week · company page: 2/week)
- **Mon — product spotlight:** one chemical, CAS, 3 applications, one spec tip; structure image or real drum photo.
- **Wed — from the floor:** export packing, QC check, container stuffing, sample dispatch (real photos).
- **Fri — buyer knowledge:** carousel cut from guides ("5 things to put in a chemical RFQ", "Incoterms explained").
- **Daily 15 min:** thoughtful comments on 5 posts by target buyers / industry pages.

### Planning funnel (assumptions to replace with actuals after 4 weeks)
280 requests/month → ~84 accepted (30%) → ~12 conversations (15%) → ~4 RFQs/samples (33%). Log every contact in a CRM sheet: date, name, company, country, segment, status (sent / accepted / replied / RFQ / sample / order).

## Meta Ads — ₹100/day (~₹3,000/month) as one focused test

**Assessment:** at this budget Meta can't efficiently find foreign procurement managers, and Meta forbids promoting hazardous goods and restricts supplement ads. Best use: **Click-to-WhatsApp ads to domestic SME buyers in industrial clusters, for non-hazardous lines.** Retarget site visitors later. One campaign, one ad set, simple targeting.

### Phase 1 (weeks 1–4, after Pixel + WhatsApp Business are ready)
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Objective | Engagement → Messaging apps: WhatsApp (Click-to-WhatsApp), number +91 98241 69906 |
| Budget | ₹100/day, one ad set, ≥ 7 days without edits (learning phase) |
| Locations | Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Ankleshwar/Bharuch, Surat, Vapi, Mumbai/Thane, Hyderabad (+25 km) |
| Audience | Age 25–60, Advantage+ with suggestions: small business owners, manufacturing, cosmetics, food processing, pharmaceutical industry |
| Products in ads | Essential oils, food preservatives (sodium benzoate, potassium sorbate), soap/detergent aroma chemicals. **Exclude** sodium metal, selenium, iodine; **no** health claims on Ayurvedic products |
| Creative (3 variants) | 1) Real photo of packed drums: "Aroma chemicals & preservatives from Ahmedabad · Since 2005 · GST-registered · Message us for today's price" 2) Carousel of 5 products with CAS numbers 3) 20-second phone video of a partner in the warehouse |
| WhatsApp greeting | "Thanks for messaging Sheetal Aromatics. Please share: 1) product 2) quantity per month 3) city 4) company name / GST. We reply within working hours." |

### Phase 2 (months 2–3)
- When the Pixel has meaningful traffic (from SEO, LinkedIn, email), move half the budget to retargeting 180-day site visitors + Instagram/Facebook engagers: "Still comparing suppliers? Request a sample against your spec." → /request-a-quote with UTM tags.
- Optional: one ad set in UAE + Kenya for preservatives and essential oils, ₹100/day for 2 weeks.

### Decision rules after 30 days
| Metric | Where | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation | Ads Manager | Track only |
| Qualified conversations (gave product, quantity, company) | WhatsApp Business labels | The real lead count |
| Cost per qualified lead | spend ÷ qualified leads | Continue if below your allowable cost per domestic lead (benchmark: 10–20% of gross margin on a typical first order) |
| Quotes & orders traced to Meta | CRM | No quotes after 60 days → stop; move the ₹3,000 to Google Search ads on long-tail CAS keywords or IndiaMART |

No credible public Indian B2B industrial benchmark for Meta CPL exists — your own 30-day numbers become the benchmark.
